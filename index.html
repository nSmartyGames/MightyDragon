Click a vehicle to select it, or hold Shift and drag a box to select several at once — the cursor becomes a target reticle. Click a tile to move there, or click an enemy to attack it; the Attack button arms targeting mode for a group, Search & Destroy sends units hunting for any nearby enemy on their own, Retreat pulls them back to the stronghold. Building buttons ask for a grass or desert tile to place on. Drag the map (without Shift) or use arrow keys to scroll, or click the minimap to jump there — fog clears as your units explore. Troopers drain 10 power points per shot, tanks drain 20 every 2 seconds (each wind turbine adds +5 to every shot) — reduce a stronghold's 10k power to 0 to eliminate that House. Harvesters unload at a silo or refinery — build one or their cargo is wasted; troopers and tanks can never enter one, only fight adjacent to it. A refinery holds as much as 4 silos and comes with a free harvester. Sandworms only have a 1-in-5 chance of swallowing a unit that shares their tile, and give up the chase after 5 seconds. A hit on your units, buildings, or stronghold auto-rallies nearby idle troops and tanks to Search & Destroy.
